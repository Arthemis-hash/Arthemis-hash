## About me

I work at the intersection of data, infrastructure and reliable software systems.

- **Data engineering:** designing practical pipelines, data models and reliable data flows.
- **Systems & networking:** building, securing and maintaining Linux-based services and networks.
- **Cloud engineering:** containerisation, deployment, reverse proxies, automation and platform operations.
- **Applied data science:** using exploration and machine learning fundamentals when they create real value.

I care about systems that are understandable, observable and maintainable — not just systems that work once.

## What I am building

- Data platforms that turn operational data into dependable, usable information.
- Self-hosted and cloud-ready infrastructure for real-world applications.
- Automation workflows that reduce repetitive work without sacrificing control.
- Developer tools and AI infrastructure with a focus on performance, resilience and responsible use.
